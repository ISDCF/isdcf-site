## Subscribing to the public mailing list

Anyone can sign-up for our public mailing list by visiting <https://lists.isdcf.com/g/isdcf>.

## Accessing member-only resources

If you and/or your organization is already a member and would like to access our member-only resources, please contact us at [info@isdcf.com](mailto:info@isdcf.com).

## Becoming a member

Membership is open to all interested parties, individuals and organizations alike. Membership allows you and your organization to participate in meetings and access member-only mailing lists and file areas. One membership covers all employees of an organization.

To become a member, please review the following membership packet and contact us at [treasurer@isdcf.com](mailto:treasurer@isdcf.com).

[ISDCF Membership Packet](./ISDCF-Membership-Packet-2023.pdf)

In March 2023, Inter-society and ISDCF implemented a new policy to allow for the sustainability of the organization going forward. Members are asked to contribute $4,000/year for organizations over $5M USD in yearly revenue and $100/year for others. ISDCF has been in operation for over 17 years with primary support by Universal Pictures. This unique organization has been able to provide significant contributions to the cinema industry through the volunteer efforts of its members. The Inter-society Board reserves the right to grant gratis membership on a case by case basis. All such memberships will be reviewed yearly by the Board.
