There will be a Celebration of Life for Jerry Pierce held in Los Angeles on the afternoon of Thursday, May 28, 2026. Please [RSVP](https://luma.com/0qw9p7vo).

We look forward to coming together to reflect on Jerry’s legacy, share stories, and celebrate his life. More details about the gathering will be shared for those who RSVP as they are confirmed.
